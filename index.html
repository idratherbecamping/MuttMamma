Training Programs & Pricing

Mutt Mama offers two distinct services. They are not two price points for the same thing — they are two different levels of involvement. The right one depends on your dog, your goals, and how much structure your household needs.

One-on-One Coaching

In-Home Private Training

$175 per 60-minute session

Pay as you go — no packages offered or required

Individualized, one-on-one training in your home and your dog's everyday environment. This is for owners who want to work directly with their own dog on specific training or behavioral issues, and who want to build their own handling skills along the way. Every session is customized to the particular dog and the issues you are actually living with.

A session may cover:
  • ✓ Manageable behavior and unwanted behaviors
  • ✓ Household structure, boundaries and responsiveness
  • ✓ Leash skills in your own neighborhood
  • ✓ Your handling and communication with your dog
  • ✓ Reasonable phone and email support between sessions
How scheduling and payment work
Training is scheduled and paid for one session at a time, with payment due in full before each session begins. Booking one session at a time keeps the plan flexible and lets us both evaluate progress after every visit.
If your dog needs more than sessions can give

For many dogs and owners, individual sessions are exactly what's needed and nothing more is required. For others, the behavior runs deeper. After roughly two in-home sessions, if you and I mutually agree that a more immersive approach would be the best way to reach your goals, you have the option of transitioning into the 21-Day Transformation Program. This is never automatic and never required — it's a decision we make together.

Ask About Availability
🐕

ONE DOG. 21 DAYS. MY FULL ATTENTION.

Mutt Mama accepts only one dog at a time into the 21-Day Transformation Program, allowing each dog to receive a truly individualized training experience while living in my home as part of my household.

This is not a kennel-style board & train. There is no run of client dogs being worked in rotation, no crate row, no shared attention. For those 21 days, your dog is my sole training client — in my home, in my routine, in my truck, on my walks, and in the real world. That is the reason this program costs what it costs, and it is the single biggest thing that separates Mutt Mama from a facility.

Because only one dog is in the program at a time, spots are genuinely limited and booked in advance.

👋

Visits Aren't Just Allowed.
They're Encouraged.

Most board & trains send you home and go quiet.

Three weeks is a long time to hand over your dog and wait. I don't want you waiting — I want you to come see it. Watching your dog change day by day is a completely different experience from hearing about it, and you should get to have it.

Visits are also working sessions. Every time you come out, you get hands-on training with your own dog while I'm standing right there to coach you. By graduation day you've already been practicing for weeks, which is exactly why the training holds up once your dog is home.

Why this matters: the single biggest reason board & train results fade is that the dog learns and the owner doesn't. Visiting closes that gap while there's still time to fix it — not in one rushed session at pick-up.

Which One Is Right for My Dog?

In-Home Private Training
  • › You want to do the training yourself, with coaching
  • › The issues are specific and situational
  • › Your household can practice consistently between visits
  • › You'd rather start small and see how it goes
  • › You want to keep your dog home throughout
21-Day Transformation
  • › Behavior is entrenched, reactive, or unsafe to manage
  • › The dog needs full-time structure to reset, not one hour a week
  • › You've tried sessions or other training without lasting change
  • › You want deep behavior modification plus owner education
  • › You're ready to follow through consistently once your dog is home

My Commitment to You

I stand behind my work completely. But I won't promise you a guaranteed outcome, and you should be skeptical of any trainer who does. Dog training depends on genetics, temperament, health, history, and above all on what happens once your dog comes home and whether the training is followed consistently. Those are not things any trainer controls.

So instead of promising a result, here is exactly what I commit to giving you:

Individualized training

A plan built for your dog specifically — and in the 21-Day Transformation, my undivided attention, because yours is the only dog in the program.

Extensive owner education

Training the dog is half the work. I teach you to handle, communicate and hold structure, because you're the one who keeps it going — starting with visits during your dog's stay.

Follow-up sessions

Two follow-ups within 60 days for 21-Day graduates, so we can adjust once real life has tested the training.

Lifetime coaching

I stay your coach for your graduating dog — reasonable guidance, questions and support, for as long as you have that dog.

A remediation option

If new issues surface down the road, your dog is eligible for a one-week remediation stay at a steeply discounted rate, subject to availability.

An ongoing community

Two Mutt Mama Social Club meetups every month, so you keep practicing with your dog alongside other trained dogs and their owners.

Mutt Mama uses balanced training principles and works with both the dog and the owner. Consistent follow-through between and after sessions is an essential part of achieving and maintaining progress.
